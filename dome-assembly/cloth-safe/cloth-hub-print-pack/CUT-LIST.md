# Rounded cloth-facing hub set

Print 5 × cloth-4-way-A, 5 × cloth-4-way-B, 6 × cloth-5-way, and 10 × cloth-6-way.
Flat face goes on the print bed and faces the INSIDE of the assembled dome.
Rounded sloping cap faces OUTSIDE against the cloth.

Use nominal 1/2-inch / 12.70 mm wooden dowels:

- 30 short dowels: **337.87 mm / 13.302 inches**
- 35 long dowels: **386.00 mm / 15.197 inches**

The blind stop is 15 mm from each mathematical node. Insertion at the socket
mouth center is 18 mm. Node centers and wood cuts are unchanged from the previous
version. Four-way A/B alternate around the base. Mark short and long connections
using the original node map; these hubs have no short-strut dimples.

Test the real dowel and cloth on one part before printing all 26 hubs.
