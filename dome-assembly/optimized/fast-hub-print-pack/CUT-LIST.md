# Optimized rib-hub cut list

Use nominal 1/2-inch / 12.70 mm wooden dowels.

- 30 short dowels: **337.87 mm / 13.302 inches**
- 35 long dowels: **386.00 mm / 15.197 inches**

Print:

- 5 × `rib-4-way-A.stl`
- 5 × `rib-4-way-B.stl`
- 6 × `rib-5-way.stl`
- 10 × `rib-6-way.stl`

The blind stop is 15 mm from each mathematical node. Socket insertion at the
mouth center is 18 mm. This differs from the old curved hubs' 27 mm insertion,
but the blind stops are unchanged, so the wooden cut lengths stay the same.

The dome node-center geometry is 26.5 inches high, 50.41 inches flat-to-flat,
and 53 inches corner-to-corner. Printed hub thickness adds to the physical
outside envelope.

The four-way A/B hubs alternate around the base. These rib hubs do not have
short-strut dimples: use the numbered assembly map and mark connections before
assembling. Print the angled socket test and verify the actual dowel fit before
printing the full set.
